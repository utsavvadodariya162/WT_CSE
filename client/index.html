Simple Process

How It Works

STEP 01

Register as Donor

Fill the simple registration form with your blood group, city and contact details. Your profile is reviewed by the administrator before listing.

STEP 02

Search or Raise Request

Search verified donors by blood group, city and availability, or submit an emergency blood request with hospital and urgency details.

STEP 03

Connect & Donate

Directly contact eligible donors, reach the hospital, and complete the blood donation to save a patient's life in need.

Eligibility Guide

  • Age 18 – 65 years
  • Weight: 50 kg or above
  • 90-day gap between donations
  • Haemoglobin 12.5 g/dL minimum
  • O− is the universal donor group
