Mission & Purpose

Finding a matching blood donor should take minutes, not hours.

When hospitals or attendants face urgent blood requirements at odd hours, frantic phone calls usually cause critical delays. RaktSetu streamlines this challenge with an organized, searchable donor directory and an active request queue.

Visitors can register as voluntary donors, filter donors by blood group and city, submit emergency requirements, and find upcoming donation camps. Administrators review donor entries, verify requests, and track blood availability across Gujarat.

Essential Knowledge

Blood Donation Awareness

FACT 01

One Unit Saves Three Lives

A single blood unit is separated into red blood cells, platelets, and plasma, enabling one donation to help three distinct patients in need.

FACT 02

Blood Cannot Be Manufactured

There is no synthetic substitute for human blood. Every unit transfused in hospitals depends solely on willing voluntary donors.

FACT 03

Quick & Safe Recovery

The human body replaces donated fluids within 24 to 48 hours and red blood cells within a few weeks. Healthy adults can safely donate every 90 days.
